Wig Care Guide

Washing

Use a sulphate-free shampoo and cool or lukewarm water. Gently work the shampoo from roots to tips — never scrub or twist. Rinse thoroughly, then apply a lightweight leave-in conditioner. Air-dry on a wig stand; avoid wringing or tumble drying.

Styling & Heat

Human hair wigs can be curled, straightened, or blow-dried just like natural hair — always apply a heat protectant spray before any tool. Keep heat below 230 °C. Synthetic wigs cannot be heat-styled unless labelled heat-resistant.

Storage

Store on a wig stand or mannequin head to maintain shape. If folding for travel, use a silk or satin bag. Keep away from direct sunlight, excess humidity, and stacking pressure. Never store damp.

Detangling & Shedding

Some shedding after washing is normal. Use a wide-tooth comb or your fingers, starting from the ends and working towards the roots. Detangle gently when the hair is slightly damp and conditioned — never force through knots dry.

Lace Care

Remove adhesive residue from the lace using rubbing alcohol and a cotton ball — work gently to avoid stretching. Do not bleach lace at home as it weakens the knots. Replace lace-front tape or glue every 1–2 weeks for a fresh hold.
