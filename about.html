Who We Are

Born in Nairobi.
Built for every woman.

Glam by Ivy started with a simple belief: every Kenyan woman deserves to feel confident in her hair. Whether you're heading to the office, a wedding in Kiambu, or just running errands in the CBD — your hair should work for you, not against you.

We offer a carefully curated selection of premium wigs, extensions, headbands, turbans, bonnets, and wig care tools — all chosen with quality and affordability in mind. From 100% human hair to beautiful synthetic styles, there's something for every occasion and every budget.

Based at HH Towers on Moi Avenue, Nairobi, we serve women across Kenya — with fast same-day delivery within Nairobi and reliable countrywide courier service.
