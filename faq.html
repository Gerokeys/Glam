FAQs

Everything you need to know about ordering from Glam by Ivy.

Delivery

Same-day or next-day delivery within Nairobi, depending on the time your order is placed. Orders placed before 1 PM are typically dispatched the same day. We'll reach out via phone or WhatsApp to confirm your delivery slot.

Payments

We accept M-Pesa (STK push directly to your phone) and card payments (Visa & Mastercard) via our secure Pesapal checkout. You can also pay manually via M-Pesa Paybill — contact us on WhatsApp for details.
Yes. M-Pesa payments are handled directly by Safaricom's secure STK push. Card payments go through Pesapal, a PCI-DSS compliant payment processor trusted by thousands of Kenyan businesses. We never store your card details.

Returns & Exchanges

We accept exchanges within 7 days of purchase, provided the item is unworn, in its original packaging, and has not been styled or chemically treated. For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns on wigs that have been worn. Contact us on WhatsApp to initiate an exchange.
We're so sorry if this happens! Please take a photo and contact us immediately via WhatsApp (+254 726 316981) or email (sales@glambyivy.com). We'll arrange a replacement or full refund as quickly as possible.

Wig Care

Yes, all our 100% human hair wigs are heat-safe up to 230°C. Semi-human wigs can handle low to medium heat (up to ~150°C). Synthetic wigs are not heat-safe unless labelled as "heat-resistant." Always use a heat protectant before styling.
For human hair: Wash gently with sulphate-free shampoo, condition well, and air dry on a wig stand. Apply wig serum to keep it shiny and tangle-free.

For synthetic: Use cool water and synthetic wig shampoo. Never use regular hair products. Store on a wig stand away from heat.

We stock wig serums and combs in our store — perfect for keeping your wig looking fresh!
Use a soft tape measure to measure your head circumference — place it at your hairline and go around to the nape of your neck and back. Most of our wigs come with adjustable straps to fit head circumferences of 53–58 cm. Not sure? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help you find the perfect fit.
